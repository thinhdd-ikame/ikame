Memory check funnel

An ikame prototype of the 16-screen funnel in testlibrary/brain-memory/funnel-content.md, in the sibling briefs' light "exam paper" look. Health-adjacent niche: for curiosity and training, not a medical test, with a support link on every screen. The five mini-games are playable.

Play it for real. All five games work: word recall, digit span, pattern, reaction and faces. Use "Demo: fill the rest" or "Skip this game" under a game to move faster. Checkpoints appear after games 2 and 4. Tap "Need help now?" on any screen to see the support sheet.
