IQ test funnel

An ikame prototype modeled on testlibrary.com: the 15-screen IQ-test funnel in testlibrary/funnel-content.md, in the brief's light "exam paper" look. Tap through it in the phone, or jump to any screen below.

Short test. The real test is 38 items in 4 sections with 3 checkpoints. This demo plays 8 original items (2 per section) so the flow stays quick. Counters read "of 8".

Faked: the 8 test items are written for the demo. The score is a fixed lookup from right answers (0–8 right → 68–125) placed on a mean-100 / SD-15 curve. There are no real norms and no age adjustment. The loading steps, Google/Apple sign-in, checkout, PDF download and share buttons are simulated, and no charge is made.

Real: prices are the brief's (testlibrary.com/pricing, 2026-09-28). The app name, domain, review quote and review platform are placeholders, so the phone never shows the real brand or its Trustpilot data. The brief's sample quote is from the real site and is not used here.

Placeholders (dashed in the phone): tests taken, review rating, reviewer name, minutes, norm group, refund window and library question counts. Fill these from real data only.

Images (img/) are AI-generated still lifes: lander, screen 2, paywall header and the next-test cards. The test items stay as SVG.

Test order is Patterns → Numbers → Shapes → Words, following the checkpoint tips. Screen 2 lists the chips in the brief's order.
