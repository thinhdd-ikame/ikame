Calmio: sleep sounds

An ikame prototype of the 22-screen funnel in funnel-content.md: a sleep quiz, a sleep profile with a personal soundscape and 4-week goals, a real 30-second listen, then a web-style paywall. Soft light theme (off-white, sage, lavender). Tap through the phone or jump to any screen. Deep link: demo.html#s17.

Safety checks: "Need help now?" is on every screen, including the paywall and the offer. Type a crisis phrase (for example "end it all") in any "Other" field. After that, closing the paywall skips the #21 offer.

One-time offer: closing #20 (x or "Not now") shows #21 once per browser session (sessionStorage ikf_offer_calmio-sleep); a second close goes straight to #22. #21 is a smaller, different product (a paid-once sound pass), not a cheaper plan. Add ?debug=1 to log events to the console.

Faked in this demo: the profile logic is a simple lookup from the problem answer, the 30-second preview is synthesised in your browser (rain, brown noise, a soft pad voice, a plain tone pair; use headphones, low volume), loading steps, email, reminders and purchases are toasts only (no charge made). Crisis detection is a keyword list, not a classifier. Helpline numbers are shown as text. Not faked: prices, renewals, rating, reviews, refund terms and the legal entity stay dashed placeholders or hidden until real values exist; nothing is invented. The four photos in img/ are calm stand-ins copied from the Calmio overthinking funnel (no people, no faces); gen_images.py makes the sleep versions. Demo email error: taken@example.com.
