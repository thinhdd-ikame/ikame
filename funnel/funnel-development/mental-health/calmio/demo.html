Calmio-style onboarding

An ikame prototype modeled on Calmio: a clickable version of the 23-screen funnel in funnel-content.md, in the brief's soft light theme (off-white, sage, lavender). Tap through it in the phone, or jump to any screen below. Deep link: demo.html#s17.

Safety checks: "Need help now?" is on every screen. Tap "Really low" on #9. Type a crisis phrase (e.g. "end it all") in any "Other" field or in the #18 chat. After any of these, closing the paywall skips the #22 offer.

One-time offer: closing #21 (× or "Not now") shows #22 once per browser session (sessionStorage ikf_offer_calmio); the second close goes straight to #23. Add ?debug=1 to log events to the console.

Collected so far

Faked in this demo: the programme and all lesson titles (the brief only gives Grief and Alcohol week titles; the rest are placeholders), the companion's replies (a scripted 2-exchange chat seeded from topic and tone), reply ideas outside Grief, the loading steps, sign-in, reminders and purchases (toasts only, no charge made). Crisis detection is a simple keyword list, not a classifier. Helpline numbers are shown as text; in the app they open the dialer or browser. Not faked: prices are the brief's US App Store list (2026-09-28). Inside the phone the app name is the placeholder {{app_name}} (SKUs: {{app_name}} Grief etc.), and rating, rating count and review cards stay placeholders. The brief's figures (Calmio: App Store 4.7 from 1.1K, Trustpilot 4.6 from 451, as of 2026-09-28) are the reference app's, not this app's. "Other" on #5 falls back to the Calm mind path, which the brief doesn't define. Demo email errors: taken@example.com, fail@example.com. Photos (img/) are AI-generated mood stand-ins with no people or faces; they are never used as reviews or social proof.
