Calmio: hypnosis for habits

An ikame prototype of the 21-screen funnel in funnel-content.md: a focus pick (drink less, quit smoking, eat calmer) with three branch questions, a care check for heavy-drinking answers, a habit profile plus "this week, 3 things", a 4-week calm plan, a 60-second sample session, then a web-style paywall. Soft light theme (off-white, sage, lavender). Tap through the phone or jump to any screen. Deep links: demo.html#s16, #s8b (care check).

Safety checks: "Need help now?" is on every screen, including the paywall and the offer. Heavy-drinking answers (#7 "Most days"/"Every day" or #8 "Sometimes"/"Often") and eating "Out of control" open a "talk to a professional" care check at #8, repeated on #16 and #19, and the #20 offer is then skipped. Type a crisis phrase (for example "end it all") in any "Other" field to see the support sheet.

One-time offer: closing #19 (x or "Not now") shows #20 once per browser session (sessionStorage ikf_offer_calmio-hypnosis); a second close goes straight to #21. The #18 sample plays at 4x speed in this demo (15 seconds for the 60-second clip). Add ?debug=1 to log events to the console.

Faked in this demo: the profile is one trigger answer mapped to four styles, the sample session is a silent progress bar with a moving waveform, loading steps, email, reminders and purchases are toasts only (no charge made). Crisis detection and the care check are keyword and answer rules, not a classifier. Helpline numbers are shown as text. Not faked: prices, renewals, the one-time offer price, rating, reviews, refund terms and the legal entity stay as dashed tokens or hidden blocks until real values exist; nothing is invented. The photos in img/ are calm stand-ins (no people, no faces); gen_images.py makes the final ones. Demo email error: taken@example.com. With no name entered, every line falls back to "you".
