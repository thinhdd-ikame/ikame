Calmio: self-discovery

An ikame prototype of the 22-screen funnel in funnel-content.md: Mara, an illustrative guide (a fictional AI character, not a person or an expert), walks you through eight short statements, an inner pattern profile with three small practices, a real short chat to rehearse one small no, then a web-style paywall. Soft light theme (off-white, sage, lavender). Tap through the phone or jump to any screen. Deep link: demo.html#s17.

Safety checks: "Need help now?" is on every screen, including the paywall and the offer. Type a crisis phrase (for example "end it all") in any "Other" field or in the #19 chat. After that, closing the paywall skips the #21 offer.

One-time offer: closing #20 (x or "Not now") shows #21 once per browser session (sessionStorage ikf_offer_calmio-self-discovery); a second close goes straight to #22. Add ?debug=1 to log events to the console.

Faked in this demo: the pattern profile is the average of each statement group mapped to four styles, Mara's replies are a scripted 2-exchange chat, loading steps, email, reminders and purchases are toasts only (no charge made). Crisis detection is a keyword list, not a classifier. Helpline numbers are shown as text. Not faked: prices, renewals, the one-time offer price, rating, reviews, refund terms and the legal entity stay as dashed tokens or hidden blocks until real values exist; nothing is invented. The photos in img/ are calm stand-ins (no people, no faces); gen_images.py makes the final ones. Mara is drawn in SVG, never a photo of a person. Demo email error: taken@example.com.
