Chai · Romance Stories

A clickable prototype of the 17-screen funnel in funnel-content.md: pick a trope, hear chapter one narrated by its lead, then reply inside the scene. It uses the Chai/ChatChi look (pink on near-black) plus the brief's candlelit story accent (amber, Lora serif captions, paper texture).

Collected so far

Demo stand-ins: story titles, leads and bios (only Kael's bio and the Ashes & Oaths lines come from the brief), every chapter-1 script, narration line, chat reply and reply idea. They're scripted and seeded from your picks, so no AI runs. Audio uses your browser's built-in speech voice (Web Speech API) when you tap ▶. Where that isn't available you get captions only, and nothing autoplays with sound. Sign-in, purchase and notification prompts are fakes (no charge is made). The Google button fails once on purpose to show the error state, and an email containing "taken" shows the second one. Prices ($99.99/year, $6.99/week, 10 free messages) are reused from the ChatChi base and need confirming. {{stories_count}}, {{app_rating}} and the review card are placeholders for real data.
