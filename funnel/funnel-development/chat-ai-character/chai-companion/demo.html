Chai Companion onboarding

A clickable prototype of the 19-screen funnel in funnel-content.md: an AI friend who remembers your day. It uses the Chai look shifted to dusk (plum and amber around the pink). "Need help now?" is on every screen, and crisis language in any typed text switches the flow to resources and removes the paywall.
