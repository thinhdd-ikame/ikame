Faked: the camera. The viewfinder is a still photo and every capture uses the same 1955 Lincoln cent. No camera is opened. The ID result, match confidence, alternatives, grade, error check and history lines are sample outputs, and the loading steps are timed. Placeholders: the brief sets no prices, value figures, trial length or review text, so those show as {{tokens}} or dashed cards. The app name, store rating, rating count and coin-database size are tokens too. The brief quotes Coinin's own listing (4.7★, 101K ratings, 270,000+ coins), and this demo must not reuse a competitor's brand or stats. Images: the jar, cloth, camera surface, coin faces and coin-type thumbnails are AI-generated illustrative photos, not real reference shots. The other-match coins (1955-D, 1955-S, 1956) are still drawings. Not built: the screen-8 coins are drawings standing in for licensed reference photos, because the image model could not render doubled letters. World and ancient swaps and the optional monthly plan are not included. Sign-in, permissions and purchases only show a toast. No charge is made and no data leaves the page.
