Quran-learning onboarding

An ikame prototype modeled on AyahPath's funnel: a clickable version of the 23-screen brief in learning/ayahpath/funnel-content.md. The app name, rating and reviews inside the phone are placeholders, not the competitor's. It uses the brief's look: deep emerald, parchment cards, muted gold, a faint 8-point geometric pattern, and Uthmani Arabic set in Amiri Quran. Tap through the phone, or jump to any screen below.
