EWA Speak funnel

An ikame prototype of the 21-screen "speaking practice with an AI tutor" web funnel in funnel-content.md, in EWA's warm look. Tap through the phone or jump to any screen. The mic test (screens 10-12) works with the mic allowed, denied or unavailable.

Flow
Collected so far

Faked for the demo: feedback is browser word matching (Web Speech recognition when the browser has it, otherwise your typed sentence), not a pronunciation model; the model voice is the browser's built-in voice; audio is not recorded or sent by this page; the CEFR estimate uses the brief's rubric; plan length uses demo values; Apple/Google sign-in, purchases and the offer are simulated and no charge is made; an email containing "taken" shows the "already has a plan" error. Placeholders, not faked: every price ({{price_4w}}...) stays a dashed token; ratings, reviews, refund days and legal entity stay hidden until a real value is set in CONFIG. Tutor avatars are stand-ins (the fox is an AI-generated still; the owl and spark avatars are emoji until gen_images.py runs). Whether EWA ships an AI speaking tutor is unverified.
