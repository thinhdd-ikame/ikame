MyGrowth scroll-swap funnel

Prototype of the 23-screen brief in learning/mygrowth-scroll-swap/funnel-content.md: the "swap doomscrolling for 5-minute lessons" angle. The result is calculated from the screen time you enter. Tap through in the phone or jump to any screen below.

Flow
Collected so far

Computed from your answers: hours per month = swap minutes x 30 / 60 (#19), the 4-week ramp of 22 swaps (#20), the knowledge-check score (#12). Hours are redirected from time you already spend, not extra free time.

Placeholders: prices, renewals and the offer price are dashed placeholder tokens (the growth team sets them), the phone-feed art, QR code and lesson-1 mockup, and one review card. Rating, review count, reviews and the money-back guarantee stay hidden until real values are set in CONFIG.

Pick "Other" on screens 3, 4, 8, 13 or 14 to see the empty-input state (CTA disabled). Try taken@example.com on #18. No purchase, sign-in or email is made.
