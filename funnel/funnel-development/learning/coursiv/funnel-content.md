# Funnel Content — Coursiv-style AI Skills Plan

Coursiv (Coursiv Limited, Limassol, Cyprus; App Store "Coursiv - AI Certificates", 4.8★ from ~3.1K ratings) is a micro-learning app for adults who feel behind on AI. It runs daily ~15-minute lessons on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Midjourney and other tools, a "28-Day AI Challenge", practice in a built-in AI playground, a 1000+ prompt library, leaderboards, and **non-accredited completion certificates** for each main guide. It acquires almost entirely through a **web quiz funnel** (`coursiv.io/dynamic`, ~2,900 live Meta/TikTok ads across ~40 page names) that sells a web subscription before install. The user logs into the app with the same email. The user gives ~14 answers about their work, AI level and goals, and gets a "Personalized AI Certificate Program" with a projected finish date. This brief is **ikame's version of that shape**. The flow comes from an adspylab capture of Coursiv's live funnel (30 screens, captured Sept 2026, plus a 46-screen Sept 2 version with checkout and downsell), cut to 25 screens. Nothing registered fits it cleanly. It is close to **personalization-quiz** (the quiz is the demo), but the output is a *dated plan plus a daily habit* rather than a one-shot reading, so it's filed as a new **learning-plan** archetype. Three things are deliberately different from Coursiv. (1) A real 60-second micro-lesson in the funnel (screen 15), so the user tastes the loop before paying. (2) Answers visibly change the plan and date; a review site (marksinsights.com) claims Coursiv sends everyone to the same courses whatever they answer. (3) No spin wheel, fake timer or name-based "promo code", and every plan card shows its renewal price. Look: this overrides the repo's dark default with a **light, professional "career" theme** (white background, deep-indigo primary, green progress and success states, real AI-tool logos as chips). The audience is 25-55 office workers, and neon-on-black reads as entertainment. Copy follows the mobile limits (headline ≤6 words, body ≤12 words), with A/B on every screen.

## Notes

**Competitor teardown (verified, adspylab capture of `coursiv.io/dynamic`, Sept 21 2026; plus Sept 2 version).** Coursiv's order: work-type tap → "2,000,000+ joined" → 16 questions (age, goal, industry, "overwhelmed by AI?", comfort, experience, blocker, career level, first use case, career fear, other courses tried, comfortable online, first-week result, daily minutes, "how would you celebrate your first results?" with money options like "Pay off some bills"), with three info bridges ("Awesome!", the HBR quote, "You're already making progress") → email → marketing opt-in → name → personal summary ("AI Skills: Above Average") → "earn your certificate by <date>" plan with before/after → feature list → proof (2M+ users, 670k+ certificates, 93% positive Trustpilot) → **"Spin & Unlock" wheel** → paywall with "50% OFF", a promo code built from the user's name and date, and a **10:00 "discount expires" timer** → checkout → downsell. Web prices seen (EUR, excl. VAT): 1-week €6.93 (struck €13.86), 4-week €19.99 → renews €39.99 every 4 weeks, 12-week €39.99 → renews €79.99 every 12 weeks. In the Sept 2 version the 1-week option was a "1-WEEK TRIAL €6.93, then auto-renews €39.99 every 4 weeks". App Store IAPs: Pro Monthly $29.99, Pro Yearly $79.99 / $39.99.

**Reference only, NOT implemented (hidden-billing exposure).** (a) The €6.93 one-week trial that rolls into a €39.99 four-week plan. The entry price, period and amount all jump at once. It is disclosed in the checkout text, but reviews (Trustpilot ~5K reviews, BBB complaints, marksinsights) describe "unexpected $39.99 charges" and charges after cancelling. (b) Post-payment add-ons with "hard-to-find skip buttons" (apollotechnical) and an unexplained extra ~$50 module charge. (c) The name-seeded "promo code" plus a resetting 10-minute timer: fake scarcity, and the 50%-off anchors are only real if the struck price is the true regular price. (d) The spin wheel. ikame's brief replaces these with: each plan renewing at its own period, renewal shown on the card and in the CTA line, a checkout receipt with the renewal date, unchecked consent boxes, no timer, no wheel, and no post-purchase add-on. If a prompt-pack add-on is ever added, it gets its own screen with an equal-weight "No thanks" and is never pre-selected.

**Also dropped from Coursiv:** the money-celebration question ("pay off bills / retirement fund"), because it implies an income outcome and invites earnings-claim scrutiny; the "You'll never worry about being replaced again" guarantee; the duplicate comfort/overwhelm questions and "comfortable learning online?" (the output doesn't use them); and the separate marketing opt-in screen (folded into an unchecked box).

**Answers must change the plan (the fix for the "same course for everyone" complaint).** Mapping: goal → plan title and paywall echo; industry → lesson examples and the micro-lesson task; experience + micro-lesson → skill meter; blocker → "You now" row; use case → Week 1 module; first win → Day 7 milestone; minutes → date. Meter rubric: experience New 0 / Tried 1 / Regular 2 / Builds 3, +1 if B picked on screen 15. 0-1 Beginner, 2-3 Explorer, 4 Practitioner. Date: 10 min → today + 35 days, 15 min → +28, 20 min → +21 (confirm against real lesson lengths).

**Certificate honesty.** Every certificate mention says "certificate of completion". It never says accredited, recognized by employers or equivalent to a degree (third-party sources describe Coursiv's as non-accredited).

**Blocks skipped:** notification opt-in (web, pre-install; reminders are set in-app on Day 1), gamified wheel (credibility), post-purchase upsell (see above), and a separate registration with a password (the email is the login; the app does magic-link or Apple/Google sign-in).

**Drop-off risk:** screen 18 (email before plan) is the cliff; screen 15 is the new risk (a lesson inside a funnel can feel like homework). Keep it one tap.

**First A/B tests:** (1) micro-lesson at 15 vs. no micro-lesson (Coursiv's shape). (2) Email gate before the plan (18) vs. after the plan, before the paywall. (3) 4-week vs. 12-week pre-selected.
