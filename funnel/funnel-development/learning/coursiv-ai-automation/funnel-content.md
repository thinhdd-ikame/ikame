# Funnel Content — Coursiv AI Automation

Niche "Tự động hoá việc lặp lại bằng AI": a Coursiv web funnel for working adults, freelancers and owners who lose hours to repeat tasks (notes, inbox, reports, proposals) and want to learn practical AI automation. The user answers ~11 taps, picks one boring task and watches AI build a three-step workflow for it in about 60 seconds. They get an estimated "hours freed per week", a 4-week plan and a dated **certificate of completion**. Archetype: **learning-plan**, 24 screens. **Modeled on:** Jobescape `jobescape.me/chat-v3` (about 15K Meta ads in 7 months, 39 screens, captured 2026-09-28; it has pivoted to a Claude-only course, so only its structure is used: use-case pain questions, authority quote, loader, profile, plan, paywall at 45) and Zenfy `zenfy.ac/get-started/0` (4.8K ads, 31 screens, captured 2026-09-28: "AI-driven income growth" profile, tools-familiar multi-select, freelancing-readiness scale, plan date chart). **What was deliberately changed:** (1) No income target question ($50k-$350k+ in Zenfy), no "what would you do with the money" question, no "AI-driven income growth" profile and no income, client or job promises anywhere, because "get rich" framing is a Meta policy risk. The goal screen lets users *say* they want freelance clients or a side project, and the next line states we teach skills, not outcomes. (2) The 60-second micro-task is real and tool-agnostic (pick one task, watch a workflow build, review it) instead of Jobescape's single-tool demo. (3) A "weekly hours lost" tap feeds one labelled, illustrative estimate rather than a made-up salary figure. (4) No expert-persona pages, no promo code, no "complete this quiz to see your subscription plan" framing, no 1-week plan that silently renews into the 4-week price, and no trademark-adjacent branding (we say "AI tools", not a vendor name, and state we are not affiliated with any tool maker). Look: the base Coursiv **light "career" theme** (indigo, white, green ticks) from the sibling briefs, with a thin "automation line" motif (trigger → AI step → you check). Copy follows the mobile limits (headline ≤6 words, body ≤12 words), with A/B on every screen.

## Notes

**Reference funnels.** Jobescape `chat-v3` (jobescape.me, 14,984 ads on the main URL over 7 months, 39 captured screens, captured 2026-09-28) and Zenfy `get-started` (zenfy.ac, 4,763 ads, 31 screens, captured 2026-09-28). Research: `funnel/research/squad1m-niches-2026-10/coursiv-coinin.md` §1. Both are verified captures. Jobescape's paywall: 1-week intro → 4-week price on renewal, 4-week pre-selected, a $1 hold; Zenfy: a $10 trial rolling to $85 per 28 days with a fake promo code. Neither is copied.

**Deliberately not implemented.** Zenfy's desired-income question ($50k-$350k+), "what would you do with the saved money", income-focused profile and "Career Freedom Challenge"; Jobescape's "complete this quiz to see your subscription plan", gender and age questions (no routing value), the "money celebration" style questions; the fake promo code; expert-persona pages; a 1-week plan that rolls into the 4-week price; a $1 card-verification hold; trademark-adjacent branding of someone else's tool.

**Meta policy guardrails ("get rich").** No screen, headline, ad line or review may promise income, clients or jobs. The goal options (freelance clients, side projects) are things the user *wants*; screen 8 says we cannot promise them. Hours freed is the only quantified benefit and is labelled illustrative. Ads must not pair the hook with income claims.

**Illustrative hours-freed estimate (unverified).** Hours band midpoint (under 2 → 1.5, 2-5 → 3.5, 5-10 → 7.5, 10+ → 12) × 0.3, rounded to the nearest half hour. The 0.3 automatable share is an assumption, not measured data: validate it against real learner data before launch or drop the number. The "40 min by hand → about 2 min" stamp on screen 13 is an example for the demo task only.

**Answers change the plan.** Work → examples and the micro-task notes. Tasks (max 3) → screen 12 default, weeks 2-3 projects. Hours → estimate. AI tools → skips basics if two or more tried (week 1 shorter). Goal → week 4 project and the paywall echo. Skills → week 4 flavour. Hurdle → week 1 style ("Don't know where to start" or "Too many tools" gets guided steps and one tool only). Pace moves the date: total of about 420 lesson-minutes, so 10 min → +42 days, 15 → +28, 20 → +21 (confirm against real lesson lengths). Band rubric: tools tried (none 0, one 1, two or more 2) + review check correct 1 + skills including "Basic code" or "Spreadsheets" 1: 0-1 Just starting, 2 Getting comfortable, 3-4 Ready to build.
