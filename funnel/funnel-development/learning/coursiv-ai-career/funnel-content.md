# Funnel Content — Coursiv AI Career Switch

Niche "AI skills cho người đổi nghề": a Coursiv web funnel for working adults changing field, returning after a career break or relocation, or rebuilding after a layoff, who want to add practical AI skills for the field they are moving into. The user answers ~11 taps (current field, target field, life event, skills they already have), sees which of five AI skills for the target field they already use, picks a **portfolio project** and watches AI outline it in about a minute, then gets a **skill gap map**, a 4-week plan and a dated **certificate of completion**. Archetype: **learning-plan**, 24 screens. **Modeled on:** Shift (about 938 Meta ads, 42 screens, captured 2026-09) and Zenfy (`zenfy.ac/get-started`, captured 2026-09-28) via `funnel/research/squad1m-niches-2026-10/coursiv-coinin.md` section 2: "career switch" hook, current and target job questions, skills, plan, long paywall. **What was deliberately changed:** (1) A **skill gap map by target field** (five concrete AI skills, "already use" vs "we'll build") replaces the competitors' generic "career profile", and one **portfolio project** is the plan's finish line. (2) No 10-minute timer, no 1:1 coaching promise, no income-target question, no "AI-driven income" profile, no promo code, no expert-persona pages. (3) Nothing promises a job, a salary, interviews or a successful switch: the plan screen says outright it is a skills plan and that hiring is up to employers. (4) Plan weeks are goals, never fixed outcomes. (5) Ratings, review counts and testimonials are unverified, so the proof screen hides them until real, attributed ikame values exist. (6) The last-chance offer is a **different product** (a one-time kit, paid once, no renewal), not a cheaper copy of a plan tier. Look: the Coursiv light "career" theme from `learning/coursiv-ai-simple` (indigo `#3B30C4`, white, green ticks) with a small "route map" motif (your field → AI skills → target field). Copy follows the mobile limits (headline ≤6 words, body ≤12 words), with A/B on every screen.

## Notes

**Reference funnels.** Shift (about 938 Meta ads, 42 screens) and Zenfy (`zenfy.ac/get-started`), via `funnel/research/squad1m-niches-2026-10/coursiv-coinin.md` section 2. The structure (hook, current job, target job, skills, plan, paywall) is taken from them; the specific screens here were written new. Per-screen details of the Shift capture beyond that research summary are **unverified**.

**Deliberately not implemented.** The 10-minute countdown, any 1:1 coaching or mentor promise, income and salary target questions, "AI-driven income" or career-change profiles, fake promo codes, expert-persona pages, a 1-week plan that rolls into the 4-week price, trademark-adjacent branding.

**Guardrails.** No screen, headline, ad line or review may promise a job, a salary, interviews or a successful switch. Plan weeks are goals. Ratings, review counts and testimonials are placeholders behind `CONFIG` and hidden until real. The guarantee line is hidden until `refundDays` is a real value. Ads must not pair the hook with income claims.

**Target field data (screens 11, 12, 18, 20).**
- Marketing & content — skills: Draft copy from a brief · Research an audience · Summarize campaign data · Make simple visuals · Plan a content calendar. Projects: A one-month content plan · A small-brand campaign brief · A campaign results summary.
- Operations & projects — skills: Summarize meetings · Draft status reports · Plan a timeline · Automate a repeat task · Spot project risks. Projects: A meeting-to-report workflow · A project plan with risks · A weekly status template.
- Customer support — skills: Draft replies · Sort incoming requests · Write a help article · Summarize a call · Spot repeat issues. Projects: A reply library · A help-article set · A repeat-issues summary.
- Data & analysis — skills: Clean a messy table · Summarize trends · Chart a result · Explain a finding in plain words · Ask better data questions. Projects: A one-page insight report · A cleaned dataset with notes · A dashboard sketch.
- Sales & outreach — skills: Research an account · Draft outreach · Summarize a call · Prepare a proposal · Write follow-ups. Projects: An outreach and follow-up kit · A proposal template · A call-notes workflow.
- Not sure yet / Other — skills: Draft text · Summarize long documents · Research quickly · Organize tasks · Check AI answers. Projects: A before/after workflow for your own job · A personal AI toolkit · A one-page "how I use AI" guide.
All five skills per field and the project lists must be matched to what the lessons really teach before launch (unverified).

**Answers change the plan.** Current field + target → route line, bridge, loader rows, examples. Life events → pacing language ("short sessions", "flexible weeks"), never outcomes.
