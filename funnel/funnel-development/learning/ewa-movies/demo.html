EWA Movies funnel

An ikame prototype of the 21-screen "learn English from your series" web funnel in funnel-content.md, in EWA's warm look. Tap through the phone or jump to any screen. Series from the ad: ?show=Friends or #s=11-show-Friends.

Flow
Collected so far

Faked for the demo: the two scenes are original illustrated stills (AI-generated stand-ins, same genre as the chosen show, never a clip from it; the suspense scene is a CSS placeholder until scene-night.jpg is generated), audio uses the browser voice if one exists, and subtitle lines, translations (Spanish, Portuguese, Turkish, Vietnamese; other languages show a stand-in) and the two 20-word lists are demo content to be replaced by a licensed subtitle frequency list. The vocabulary estimate is computed from your taps with the brief's rubric (made-up words count against you); plan length uses demo values. Apple/Google sign-in, purchases and the offer are simulated and no charge is made; an email containing "taken" shows the "already has a plan" error. The funnel UI doesn't switch into the native language. Placeholders, not faked: every price ({{price_4w}}...), rating, review, refund days and legal entity stay as dashed tokens. Series names are plain text, with no posters or logos. The brief cites EWA's own figures (4.7 stars / 196K ratings) as reference only.
