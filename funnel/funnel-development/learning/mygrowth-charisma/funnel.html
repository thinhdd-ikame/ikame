MyGrowth charisma funnel

Prototype of the 21-screen brief in learning/mygrowth-charisma/funnel-content.md: "never run out of words". Three simulated conversations with feedback decide your conversation style and your script cards. Tap through in the phone or jump to any screen below.

Flow
Collected so far

Computed from your answers: style = the tags of your three replies (2+ questions = Listener, 2+ stories = Storyteller, otherwise Connector) (#9, #16); script cards come from your first tough situation (#18); weekly themes from your style skills and goal (#17); hours = 20 cards x minutes / 60. Weeks are goals, not promised outcomes.

Placeholders: prices, renewals, the offer price, rating and review blocks and the guarantee are gated in CONFIG and stay hidden until real values are set. Illustrations are flat placeholders until gen_images.py runs; QR and phone mockups are placeholders.

Pick "Other" on screens 3, 4 or 10 to see the empty-input state (CTA disabled). Try taken@example.com on #15. No purchase, sign-in or email is made.
