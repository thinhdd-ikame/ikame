AyahPath: Quranic Arabic funnel

Prototype of the 21-screen brief in learning/ayahpath-arabic/funnel-content.md: "understand what you recite". Six Al-Fatiha words decide the "% of Al-Fatiha you know", your pace sets a goal date. Tap through in the phone or jump to any screen below.

Flow
Collected so far

Computed from your answers: the % counts every word of Al-Fatiha (29, repeats counted each time, Bismillah included) that belongs to a word you answered correctly (#7-#12); unchecked words are not counted, and lucky guesses may count. Words to learn = distinct words left (23 minus the ones you know). Days = words left / words per day (minutes / 2.5, an assumption) plus letter days if you cannot read yet. Dates are goals, not promised outcomes.

Needs review before launch: every Arabic string, transliteration, meaning and the word counts must be checked by a qualified scholar and an Arabic linguist. Arabic is live text (Amiri Quran), never baked into images.

Placeholders: prices, renewals, the offer price, rating and review blocks and the guarantee are gated in CONFIG and stay hidden until real values are set. Pictures are generated placeholders until gen_images.py runs; QR is a placeholder.
