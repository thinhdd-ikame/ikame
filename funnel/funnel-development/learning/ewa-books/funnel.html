EWA Books funnel

An ikame prototype of the 21-screen "read real books in English" web funnel in funnel-content.md, in EWA's warm look. Tap through the phone or jump to any screen. Genre from the ad: ?genre=Mystery or #s=8-genre-Mystery.

Flow
Collected so far

Faked for the demo: the book covers, hook photo and passage illustration are wordless stand-ins copied from learning/ewa (run gen_images.py for books-specific art); book titles are generic sample titles, not real books. The two passages, translations (Spanish, Portuguese, Turkish, Vietnamese; other languages show a stand-in) and the level estimate are demo content: the level starts from your self-rating and moves one step with "How did that read?", and plan length uses demo values. Audio uses the browser voice if one exists. Apple/Google sign-in, purchases and the offer are simulated and no charge is made; an email containing "taken" shows the "already has a plan" error. The funnel UI doesn't switch into the native language. Hidden while unset, not faked: app rating, review count, reviews, refund days and the guarantee stay out of the page until real values are set in CONFIG (screen 13 shows a neutral progress headline instead). Every price ({{price_4w}}...) and the legal entity stay as dashed tokens. The brief cites EWA's own figures (4.7 stars / 196K ratings) as unverified reference only.
