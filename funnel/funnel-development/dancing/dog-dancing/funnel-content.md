# Funnel Content — Dog Dancing

AI video generator funnel: dog owner uploads a photo of their dog, AI turns it into a fun dancing video. 12-screen flow, modeled on the reference funnel (hook → personalization quiz → generation → social proof → paywall → payment → success). Copy kept short to fit mobile screens.

---

**Purpose:** Grab attention with an emotional "before" state and introduce the promise.
**Headline:** Your dog, now dancing
**Body:** Turn a photo into a fun video.
**Visual:** Dark background, rounded card with photo collage of dogs, trophy badge top bar, purple gradient CTA button.
**CTA:** Continue
