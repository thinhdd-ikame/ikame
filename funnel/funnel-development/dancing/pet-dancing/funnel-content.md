# Funnel Content — Pet Dancing

AI video generator funnel: pet owner uploads a photo of their pet, AI turns it into a fun dancing video. 12-screen flow, modeled on the reference funnel (hook → personalization quiz → generation → social proof → paywall → payment → success).

---

## 1. Welcome Hook A
**Purpose:** Grab attention with an emotional "before" state and introduce the promise.
**Headline:** Turn your pet's photo into a dancing video in seconds
**Body:** Your favorite pet photo, brought to life — cute, funny, shareable.
**Visual:** Background collage of pet photos (dogs & cats).
**CTA:** Continue

## 2. Welcome Hook B
**Purpose:** Reinforce the promise with social framing (relatable use case).
**Headline:** Follow the steps, and it's ready
**Body:** Upload a photo, answer a few quick questions, and get your pet's dancing video.
**Visual:** Two overlapping pet photos.
**CTA:** Continue

## 3. Welcome Hook C
**Purpose:** Set expectation for time savings / ease.
**Headline:** Remember this moment, and enjoy it forever
**Body:** In just a minute, turn a still photo into a moving memory.
**Visual:** Single pet photo, warm tone.
**CTA:** Continue

## 4. Q1 — Occasion / Goal
**Purpose:** Segment intent, increase investment before the ask.
**Headline:** What's the occasion?
**Options:**
- 🎉 Pet's birthday
- 🐾 Just for fun
- 🎁 Gift for a pet lover
- 📱 Post on social media
**CTA:** Continue

## 5. Q2 — Pet's Name
**Purpose:** Personalize the rest of the funnel with the pet's name.
**Headline:** What's your pet's name?
**Field:** Text input, placeholder "Enter name"
**CTA:** Continue

## 6. Q3 — Dance Style
**Purpose:** Let user pick the dance style used in generation (personalization + perceived control).
**Headline:** What kind of dance would {{pet_name}} love?
**Options:**
- 🐕 Funny & Bouncy
- 🐈 Smooth & Sassy
- 🔥 Viral TikTok Style
- 🎶 Surprise me
**CTA:** Continue

## 7. Video Generation (Loading)
**Purpose:** Build anticipation while AI "works," reduce perceived wait.
**Headline:** Creating {{pet_name}}'s dance video...
**Body:** Analyzing photo → applying motion → rendering video
**Visual:** Animated grid of pet photos morphing, progress bar.
**CTA:** (auto-advances)

## 8. Testimonial / Social Proof
**Purpose:** Build trust right before the reveal/paywall.
**Headline:** 1,486,000+ dancing videos created
**Body:** Loved by pet owners everywhere.
**Visual:** Download counter + press logos (placeholder: Forbes, TechCrunch, The Dodo).
**CTA:** Continue

## 9. Landing Hug (Emotional Preview)
**Purpose:** Show a blurred/teaser preview of the result to trigger desire before the paywall.
**Headline:** {{pet_name}}'s video is ready!
**Body:** Sign up to watch and download it.
**Visual:** Blurred video thumbnail of the pet, dark overlay.
**CTA:** Continue

## 10. Registration
**Purpose:** Hard gate — capture email before showing the result.
**Headline:** Create your account to continue
**Fields:** Email, Password
**CTA:** Continue

## 11. Payment
**Purpose:** Convert to paid plan to unlock the video/download.
**Headline:** Unlock {{pet_name}}'s dancing video
**Body:** Choose your plan
**Plans:** Weekly / Annual (highlight savings on annual)
**CTA:** Continue with Apple Pay / Continue

## 12. Success / Download
**Purpose:** Deliver the payoff, drive sharing (viral loop).
**Headline:** Your video is ready!
**Body:** Download or share {{pet_name}}'s dancing video.
