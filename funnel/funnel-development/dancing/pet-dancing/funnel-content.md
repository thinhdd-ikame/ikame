# Funnel Content — Pet Dancing

AI video generator funnel: pet owner uploads a photo of their pet, AI turns it into a fun dancing video. 12-screen flow, matching the Figma flow board (hooks → name → style → upload → social proof → generation → lucky wheel → email → paywall). Copy kept short to fit mobile screens.

**Body:** Turn one photo into a funny video.
**Visual:** Dark background, rounded card with collage of dogs and cats, trophy badge top bar, purple gradient CTA button.
