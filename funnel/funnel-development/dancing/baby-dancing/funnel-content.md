# Funnel Content — Baby Dancing

AI video generator funnel: parent uploads a photo of their baby, AI turns it into a fun dancing video. 12-screen flow, modeled on the reference funnel (hook → personalization quiz → generation → social proof → paywall → payment → success).

---

**Purpose:** Grab attention with an emotional "before" state and introduce the promise.
**Headline:** Turn your baby's photo into a dancing video in seconds
**Body:** Your favorite baby photo, brought to life — cute, funny, shareable.
**Visual:** Background collage of baby photos.
**CTA:** Continue

**Headline:** Follow the steps, and it's ready
**Body:** Upload a photo, answer a few quick questions, and get your baby's dancing video.

**CTA:** Continue

**CTA:** Continue
