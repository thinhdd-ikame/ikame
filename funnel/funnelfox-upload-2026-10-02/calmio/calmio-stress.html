Calmio: stress reset

An ikame prototype of the 22-screen funnel in funnel-content.md: a stress-pattern quiz, a pattern plus a 4-week micro-reset plan, a real 2-minute reset chat, then a web-style paywall. Soft light theme (off-white, sage, lavender). Tap through the phone or jump to any screen. Deep link: demo.html#s17.

Safety checks: "Need help now?" is on every screen, including the paywall and the offer. Type a crisis phrase (for example "end it all") in any "Other" field or in the #19 chat. After that, closing the paywall skips the #21 offer.

One-time offer: closing #20 (x or "Not now") shows #21 once per browser session (sessionStorage ikf_offer_calmio-stress); a second close goes straight to #22. Add ?debug=1 to log events to the console.
