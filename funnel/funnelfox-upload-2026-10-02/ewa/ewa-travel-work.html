EWA Travel and Work funnel

An ikame prototype of the 19-screen "English before your trip, interview or new job" web funnel in funnel-content.md, in EWA's warm look. Tap through the phone or jump to any screen. Event from the ad: ?event=interview or #s=8-event-job.

Flow
Collected so far

Faked for the demo: the three situations per event, the phrase kits and the level rubric are original demo content to be reviewed by the content team; scene art is CSS placeholder art until scene-*.jpg are generated (drop them in img/ and the IMG map); audio uses the browser voice if one exists. The "ready by" date and day counts are real calendar maths from today; plan length and goals use demo values and are goals, never promises. Apple/Google sign-in, purchases and the offer are simulated and no charge is made; an email containing "taken" shows the "already has a plan" error. Placeholders, not faked: every price ({{price_4w}}...), refund days and legal entity stay as dashed tokens; ratings and reviews are tokens and their blocks stay hidden until a real value is set in CONFIG.
