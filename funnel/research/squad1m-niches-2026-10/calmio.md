## Cross-funnel patterns
- Spine template: gender/age hero → symptom Likert block → myth/info interstitials → expert/CBT authority → email → named profile/score → loader with micro-questions → (scratch card) → 3-plan paywall.
- Pricing: 3 plans (1W/4W/12W or 1M/3M/6M), 50-80% intro off, renews at the "was" price, 10-15 min timer, conditional 30-day money-back.
- Result objects: severity scores (Innerflo 32/100), stress gauges (Calm, Liven), personality labels (Chillio), candidacy verdicts (Hypnozio).
